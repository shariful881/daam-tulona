    Buying a Laptop in 2025 – What to Consider

    When choosing a laptop, look for the right balance of performance, design, and price. Key factors include:

    Processor (CPU)

    The CPU is the most crucial component.

    • High-end tasks: Core i7, i9, Core Ultra 7/9, Ryzen 7, Ryzen 9
    • Balanced performance: Core i5, Core Ultra 5, Ryzen 5
    • Basic tasks: Core i3, Ryzen 3

    Apple laptops use M1, M2, and A-series chips offering excellent performance and efficiency.

    Display

    Choose your display size based on your needs:

    • 13–14 inches: Ideal for students and travelers
    • 15 inches: Best for professionals and everyday users
    • 16–17 inches: Preferred by gamers, editors, and creators

    A minimum Full HD (1080p) display is recommended for all workloads.

    Material

    Laptop bodies are generally made of plastic or metal:

    • Plastic: Lightweight, budget-friendly
    • Metal (Aluminum): More durable, premium feel, slightly better heat dissipation

    RAM

    More RAM means smoother multitasking:

    • Minimum: 4 GB (basic use)
    • Recommended: 8 GB (general use)
    • Professional work: 16 GB or more (editing, rendering, programming)

    Modern laptops use DDR4 or DDR5 memory.

    Storage

    Choose between HDD and SSD:

    • HDD: Affordable, high capacity
    • SSD: Much faster, improves boot and loading times
    • NVMe M.2 SSD: The fastest storage option

    A practical setup is combining a small SSD (for OS) and a large HDD (for bulk files).

    Battery

    Expect:

    • 3–4 hours for heavy tasks
    • 5–6 hours for light usage

    Ultrabooks offer great battery life while keeping the device ultra-light.

    Connectivity

    Important connectivity features:

    • WiFi 6E for faster wireless internet
    • HDMI for external display
    • USB Type-C / Thunderbolt for fast charging and data transfer
    • USB 3.1 ports for speed
    • SD card slot for camera, drone, and action cam users

    Upgrading Laptop RAM, Storage & Graphics

    Most laptops allow upgrades for RAM and storage (HDD/SSD).

    • A 2.5-inch SATA SSD/HDD fits into the SATA port.
    • NVMe SSDs require an M.2 slot.
    • RAM upgrades depend on free RAM slots and supported BUS speed.
    • Most laptop GPUs are soldered and cannot be upgraded.
